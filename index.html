05 What teachers experience

A sample of what your staff will learn to do.

Activities teachers take back to their classrooms the very next day.

01

Fluency

5-Day Reader’s Theatre

Cold read and rate. Echo and choral reading. Rehearse. Perform. A structured, zero-prep protocol that builds prosody and engagement together.

02

Vocabulary · Morphology

Would You Rather: Greek & Latin Roots

Students debate two playful scenarios using the meaning of a morpheme—building word-analysis skills without losing the joy of the lesson.

03

Phonics · Writing · Nonfiction

Synergistic Story Building

Students decode compound words, research a nonfiction topic, draft a narrative, and perform it: four standards, one connected lesson arc.
