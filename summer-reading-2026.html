About the Program

A 6-Week Literacy Adventure for Your Family

Welcome to a 6-week literacy adventure designed to bring more laughter, conversation, imagination, and connection into reading at home.

This isn't about worksheets or drills. It's about helping kids experience reading as something that is fun, social, expressive, meaningful, and worth doing every day.
